# AI Bias Case Study in India

## Case
UIDAI Facial Recognition System

## Background
India's UIDAI uses facial recognition technology for identity verification. Studies have reported lower recognition accuracy for individuals with darker skin tones and under varying lighting conditions.

## Root Cause
- Representation bias in the training dataset.
- Insufficient diversity across skin tones.
- Limited real-world environmental conditions during model training.

## Impact
- Higher authentication failures.
- Reduced accessibility for affected users.
- Potential discrimination against certain demographic groups.

## Proposed Solution
- Increase diversity in training data.
- Perform regular fairness audits.
- Use fairness metrics during model evaluation.
- Continuously monitor model performance after deployment.

## Conclusion
Responsible AI practices, fairness testing, and representative datasets are essential to ensure equitable AI systems for all users.
