Week 11 Day 4 - RAG Evaluation and Optimization

Baseline Evaluation:
- Evaluation dataset: 10 questions
- Metrics: Faithfulness, Answer Relevancy, Context Precision, Context Recall
- Context Recall: 1.0 for all 10 questions
- Faithfulness and Answer Relevancy: Scores varied across questions
- Context Precision: Mostly high, with low scores for some questions

Observations:
- Some generated answers may not be fully supported by the retrieved context.
- Some answers may not directly address the question.
- Context Recall was high, so retrieval coverage appears sufficient for this dataset.
- Low scores should be reviewed alongside the original answers and retrieved contexts.

Optimization Plan:
1. Review low-scoring answers.
2. Check whether the retrieved context supports each answer.
3. Improve the answer-generation prompt where needed.
4. Compare the updated results with the baseline.
5. Keep the same 10 evaluation questions for a fair comparison.

Conclusion:
The baseline RAG evaluation was completed using Ragas.
The results identify answer faithfulness and relevance as areas for further investigation.
