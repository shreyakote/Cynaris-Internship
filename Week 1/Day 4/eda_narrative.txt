The dataset contains information about students including their 
ID, name, state, course, and marks. Initial inspection using info() and describe() 
shows that the dataset is small, well-structured, and easy to understand. 
The isnull().sum() function indicates that there are no missing values, 
making the dataset suitable for analysis without additional cleaning. 
The distribution of marks shows that most students scored between 75 and 95,
 indicating generally good academic performance. 
 The category count plot shows that AIML is the most common course among the students, 
 followed by Data Science and Cyber Security. 
 The correlation heatmap provides relationships between the numeric columns. 
 Since this dataset contains only a few numeric features, 
 the correlation is limited but still useful for identifying relationships. 
 One suspicious aspect is the small size of the dataset, 
 which may not be sufficient for training a machine learning model. 
 Collecting more student records from different states and courses would improve the quality of future analysis. Additional features such as attendance, semester GPA, and placement status could also make the dataset more valuable for predictive modeling.
